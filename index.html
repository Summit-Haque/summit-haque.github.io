Research

Human–computer interaction, computing education, software engineering, and artificial intelligence.

Assessing individual contributions in team software projects

FRONTIERS, an NSF-funded collaborative project

Team projects are central to software engineering courses, but grading each student's share of the work is hard. We develop and evaluate ways to assess the quality of individual contributions from the artifacts teams already produce: commits, issues, pull requests, and code reviews. I contributed to a Delphi study defining what makes a good commit, which won the Best Paper Award at CSEE&T 2026. A follow-up paper is under review at ICSE (SEET) 2027.

Generative AI in programming problem solving

Comparative study, United States and Bangladesh

How do undergraduate computing students actually use generative AI while solving programming problems, and what does it do to their learning? I compare students in the US and Bangladesh to understand differences in access, habits, and outcomes. A paper from this work is under review at ICSE (SEET) 2027.
